Privacy policy

This policy covers the SilentFit Android app (on Google Play and on GitHub) and this website. SilentFit is developed by Hariharan Mudaliar ("I", "me").

In short: SilentFit does not collect any personal data. The app has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics. Everything it reads stays on your phone, except what it sends to your own watch over Bluetooth. I never receive any of your data.

All of the following is processed only on your phone and, where noted, on your paired watch. None of it is sent to me or to anyone else.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): find, pair with and stay connected to the watch. SilentFit does not use Bluetooth to find your location.
  • Notifications: show the connection status and the find-my-phone alert.
  • Notification access: read notifications and the media session so they can be shown on the watch. SilentFit explains this and asks you before opening Android's setting.
  • Phone: answer or decline calls from the watch.
  • SMS (GitHub build only): send a quick reply from the watch. The Google Play build does not request this permission.
  • Camera: read the pairing QR code.
  • Alarms: copy watch alarms to your phone's clock app when you turn that on.
  • Foreground service and start at boot: keep the watch connected in the background.

Sharing

SilentFit does not share, sell or transfer any data to me or to third parties. It contains no advertising or analytics code. Links in the app, such as the source code, my profile or Buy Me a Coffee, open in your browser, and those sites are covered by their own policies.

Storage and security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Android backup is turned off for SilentFit, so its data is not copied to cloud backups. The link to the watch uses Bluetooth pairing, which is encrypted by Android.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can:

  • clear health history in Settings › Data › Clear health history;
  • erase everything SilentFit stored in Settings › Data › Delete all data;
  • uninstall the app, which removes all of its data.

Data held on the watch itself is managed by the watch. A factory reset of the watch removes it.

Children

SilentFit is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly process any child's data. Because the app collects no data, there is nothing to delete on my side.

This website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address when you visit; see GitHub's privacy statement. The site uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. Your theme choice and any watch face draft in the Studio are saved only in your browser's local storage.
